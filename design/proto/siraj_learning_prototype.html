WORKPLACE HEALTH & SAFETY · LESSON 3 OF 4
Module 3 · 8 min

Make safety part of the way you work.

A safer workplace is built through small, consistent actions. Knowing what to look for is the first step; acting when you notice a risk is what makes the difference.

Pause. Notice. Act.

Before starting a task, take a moment to check your surroundings. Look for trip hazards, damaged equipment, blocked exits or anything that feels out of place. If you spot a risk, follow your site procedure and let the right person know.

Remember
Never assume a hazard has already been reported. If you see something unsafe, make it safe only if you can do so without putting yourself or others at risk, then report it.

Three habits that make a difference

Stay aware. Keep your attention on the task and the environment around you.
Use the right equipment. Follow training and wear the required protective equipment.
Speak up. Report near misses and unsafe conditions promptly.

These actions help protect you, your colleagues and everyone who visits your workplace.

A Walk around it and continue with your task.
B Warn others, make the area safe if possible, and report it.
C Wait to see whether someone else reports it.
D Clean it immediately, regardless of the substance.
