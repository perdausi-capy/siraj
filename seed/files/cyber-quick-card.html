Cyber Security Basics · Quick reference

Stop, check, report

Keep this card by your desk. Most attacks need you to click, open or share something. A few seconds of checking stops them.

Signs of a phishing message

  • Urgent pressure or a threat
  • A sender address that is almost right
  • Links that don't match the text
  • Unexpected invoices or attachments
  • Requests for passwords, codes or payments

Strong sign-in

  • Use a passphrase of four or more random words
  • A different password for every account
  • Keep passwords in the company password manager
  • Always turn on multi-factor sign-in
  • Never share a sign-in code, even with IT

Do

  • Lock your screen when you step away
  • Check payment changes by phone, on a known number
  • Store files in approved company systems
  • Install updates when you are asked to

Don't

  • Plug in USB sticks you found or were given
  • Send company data to personal email
  • Approve sign-in prompts you didn't start
  • Discuss confidential work in public places

If something looks wrong

1Don't click, reply or open anything else.
2Use the Report button, or forward it to the IT security desk.
3Clicked already? Report it within the hour. Fast reports limit the damage.
