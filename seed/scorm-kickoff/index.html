1 of 3 · Why kick-off matters

Start every project on the same page

A kick-off meeting turns a plan on paper into a shared commitment. In 45 minutes the team should leave knowing the goal, their part in it, and how success will be measured.

Rule of thumb: if two people describe the goal differently after the kick-off, it is not done yet.
2 of 3 · The agenda

Five things every kick-off covers

  1. The goal and why it matters to the business
  2. Scope: what is in, and what is out
  3. Roles: who decides, who does, who is kept informed
  4. Milestones and the first two weeks of work
  5. Risks, and how the team will raise problems early
3 of 3 · After the meeting

Send a one-page summary the same day

Write down decisions, owners and dates while they are fresh, and ask everyone to reply if anything is wrong. That page becomes the reference for the rest of the project.
